Thoth is an open-source font for Egyptian hieroglyphs, made by the Bastet project.

It is named after Thoth (Egyptian ḏḥwtj, Djehuty), the god who in Egyptian belief gave people the hieroglyphic script.

Thoth arranges signs into quadrats (stacked and grouped signs) using the Unicode Egyptian Hieroglyph Format Controls (U+13430 to U+1343F), so text written in Unicode looks the way it does on monuments and in grammar books.

The sign outlines come from Noto Sans Egyptian Hieroglyphs, designed by the Monotype Design Team for the Noto project.

To contribute or report an issue, please visit github.com/bastet00/thoth.
